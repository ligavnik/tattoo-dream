// Our Story

ABOUT VOID INK

Founded in 2009 by master tattooers who believed that tattoo artistry deserved the same reverence as fine art, Void Ink has grown from a two-chair studio into one of the most respected tattoo collectives on the East Coast.

We don't just apply ink — we translate your stories, passions, and memories into permanent art. Every consultation is collaborative. Every design is original. Every session is a ceremony.

Our artists collectively bring over 80 years of combined experience across realism, blackwork, fine line, traditional Japanese, watercolor, geometric, and everything in between.

✦

HYGIENE & SAFETY STANDARDS

Single-Use Needles

All needles are sterile, single-use, and disposed of immediately after each session.

Medical-Grade Sterilization

All reusable equipment is processed through an autoclave between every client.

Licensed & Inspected

Fully licensed studio with regular health department inspections. All artists hold current bloodborne pathogen certifications.

Vegan-Friendly Inks

We use only vegan, cruelty-free, high-grade tattoo inks from certified suppliers.
